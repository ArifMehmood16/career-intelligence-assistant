# Licensing research — 2026-10-08

## Current human decision

Internal business use and closed-source modifications are allowed, including in
profitable companies. Free public products are allowed if unmonetised. External
sales, subscriptions, paid services and advertising/sponsorship require written
permission. Keep original copyright and specify accessible attribution locations.
This supersedes both the initial strict custom draft and the standard PolyForm
checkpoint on the same unmerged PR.

[Official PolyForm Noncommercial](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0)
uses noncommercial/personal purposes and listed organisational exceptions. It is
not an express blanket permission for all internal business use. Use independently
written custom terms with their own name, rather than label changed conditions as
unchanged PolyForm. The [publisher's terms](https://github.com/polyformproject/polyform-licenses/blob/1.0.0/README.md)
also require removing its name from modified templates.

[UK IPO licensing guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/license-and-sell-your-copyright)
distinguishes permission to use from copyright assignment. Fees/revenue share
require an agreement for rights controlled by the grantor; the public grant does
not automatically transfer another person's revenue. [Ownership guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works)
explains creator/joint ownership and required owners' permissions. Retain rights
in original copied code without claiming contributors' original additions.

The [Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html)
distinguishes expression from ideas. Independent ideas-based implementations are
outside this project's copied-code restrictions. Existing valid grants remain
applicable to the versions offered under them; do not claim retroactive withdrawal.

Implementation choices: internal paid employees/contractors are allowed on behalf
of the internal user; a paid client service delivering the software's functionality
requires permission. No source-publication obligation. Retain notices, show credit
in external About/Credits/Legal locations and accompanying public documentation;
no splash screen or per-output credit requirement. Third-party fonts retain SIL OFL
and Spec Kit retains upstream MIT. No automatic patent/trademark transfer, royalty
percentage, contributor assignment or legal-enforceability claim.
