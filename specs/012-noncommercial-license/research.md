# Licensing research — 2026-10-08

Existing LICENSE identifies Arif Mehmood and prohibits all reuse. README says
proprietary. No application package currently declares a conflicting licence.
Fonts retain SIL OFL; Spec Kit retains its upstream MIT notice.

Decision: custom noncommercial software terms, not an open-source label. The
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires commercial use and
resale rights, which conflict with the human's explicit restriction. This document
is an implementation record, not a professional opinion on enforceability.

The [UK Intellectual Property Office guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/license-and-sell-your-copyright)
explains that copyright owners may license use and choose its terms; ownership
transfer is separate. Scope grants to rights held by the owner/contributors and
preserve other parties' terms. Do not silently assign incoming contributions.

The [US Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html)
distinguishes protected expression from ideas. Explicitly permit learning and
independent ideas-based implementations while retaining restrictions on copied
code and protected derivatives. Do not claim exclusive rights to the concept.

Alternatives rejected: retaining no-use terms blocks collaboration; permissive or
copyleft open-source terms permit commercial reuse; describing a custom restriction
as a standard licence would mislead recipients. No change to third-party licences,
no patent/trademark grant and no assumed jurisdiction are added.
