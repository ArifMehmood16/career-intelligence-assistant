# Licensing research — 2026-10-08

Initial custom draft f1d1f4e is superseded by the human's selection of unchanged
PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 after the standard exceptions were explained.

Decision: [official PolyForm text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0.txt),
kept byte-identical in LICENSE. Its Notices section supports a separate Required
Notice naming Arif. Noncommercial Organizations expressly permits listed bodies
regardless of funding. Patent License and violation/cure provisions are preserved.
No Other Rights permits the owner to grant separate licences. No royalty or revenue
share obligation exists in this standard public licence.

The [PolyForm publisher's terms](https://github.com/polyformproject/polyform-licenses/blob/1.0.0/README.md)
allow reuse of the template, and require removing its name if its terms are changed.
Keeping exact official text avoids custom restrictions masquerading as PolyForm.

[UK IPO licensing guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/license-and-sell-your-copyright)
distinguishes licensing use from copyright assignment. Commercial fees or a revenue
share can be negotiated separately for rights controlled by the grantor; they are
not automatic claims on every user's earnings.
[Ownership guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works)
explains creator/joint ownership and the need for required owners' permission.
Retain contributors' rights rather than claiming every addition belongs to Arif.

The [Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html)
distinguishes expression from ideas. Independent ideas-based work is not converted
into a copyrighted derivative or royalty obligation by this repository's policy.

Third-party fonts retain SIL OFL and Spec Kit retains upstream MIT. This research is
a source-backed implementation record, not an opinion that any dispute would yield
all revenue or that any particular use is necessarily noncommercial.
